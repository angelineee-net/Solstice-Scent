Meet the founder

Lindsay Margarette Illagan

“Solstice started with a simple idea: create something practical, beautiful, and meaningful from a student's own experience.”

Lindsay Margarette Illagan began Solstice Candle as a student who wanted to build something of her own while studying and working toward her goals. Through her experience with warm evenings and mosquitoes, she discovered an opportunity to turn a simple candle into a useful everyday product.

What began as a student-led idea grew into Solstice Candle — a brand centered on thoughtful scents, natural soy wax, and a product designed to make evenings feel more comfortable.

Distribution management

From production to your doorstep.

Solstice Candle's distribution system is designed to keep orders organized, products protected, and customers updated from purchase through delivery.

01

Inventory

Track candle stock by scent, prioritize the Lemongrass main blend, and maintain a ready-to-ship supply.

02

Order Processing

Confirm the customer's scent, quantity, promo eligibility, payment, and delivery information before dispatch.

03

Packaging & Dispatch

Pack candles securely, label each order, and coordinate delivery through the appropriate local courier or pickup arrangement.

04

Customer Follow-up

Provide order updates and collect customer feedback to improve product quality, service, and future distribution.

Distribution channels

Direct sales: Website inquiries, school/community selling, and direct customer orders.

Local delivery: Courier-based delivery within Metro Manila and nearby areas, subject to availability and delivery fees.

Pickup: Coordinated pickup at the listed business location when available.

Future expansion: Campus ambassadors, reseller partners, pop-up booths, and selected retail partners.

Order fulfillment management

Orders are checked against available inventory by scent, packed securely, labeled, and prepared for customer pickup or delivery. Customers receive confirmation and delivery updates, while feedback is collected after purchase to support continuous improvement.
